Kidney Filters

Your kidney tests

Your kidneys are a water treatment plant with about a million tiny sieves. They clean waste out of the blood and keep the useful things in.

What we check

eGFR in mL/min/1.73m² and ACR in mg/mmol, using KDIGO categories as in NICE NG203. CKD is diagnosed only when results stay abnormal for more than 3 months; a new low eGFR should be repeated within 2 weeks. eGFR can dip with dehydration, a large meat meal or heavy exercise the day before, and reads differently with high or low muscle mass. An ACR of 3 or more should be confirmed on an early morning sample. NICE offers an ACE inhibitor or ARB with diabetes and ACR over 3, with hypertension and ACR over 30, and for anyone with ACR of 70 or more. Simplified for explaining results.
