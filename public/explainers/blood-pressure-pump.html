Blood Pressure Pump

Your blood pressure

Your heart is a pump and your arteries are stretchy hoses. Blood pressure is how hard the blood pushes on the hose walls.

What we check

NICE NG136: clinic 140/90 or more is confirmed with home or ambulatory readings; home average 135/85 or more is stage 1, 150/95 or more is stage 2. Treatment target under 140/90 in clinic (135/85 at home), under 150/90 if aged 80 or over. In middle age, each 20 mmHg higher systolic roughly doubles the risk of dying from heart disease or stroke (Prospective Studies Collaboration, Lewington 2002). Typical systolic falls: salt under 6 g a day about 5, about 1 per kg lost, regular aerobic exercise about 4 to 5, cutting heavy drinking about 3 to 5, one standard-dose tablet about 9 (Law 2009). Effects vary and roughly add up.
