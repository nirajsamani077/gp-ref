Insulin Resistance Cycle

How weight, insulin and sugar are linked

Spare energy is stored as fat in a cupboard under the skin. Insulin is the key that lets sugar into cells. Step through to see what happens when the cupboard overflows.

What we check

Lifestyle change reduced progression from prediabetes to diabetes by 58% in the Diabetes Prevention Program (Knowler 2002). In DiRECT, nearly half of people with type 2 diabetes for under 6 years were in remission at 1 year after a structured weight-loss programme (Lean 2018).
