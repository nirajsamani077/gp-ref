Fatty Liver Explained

Fatty liver

Your liver is the body's factory. It is made of millions of small cells, and each one can fill up with fat when there is more energy than the body needs.

What we check

Now called MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease), previously NAFLD. ALT can be normal even with significant fatty liver. FIB-4 = (age × AST) ÷ (platelets × √ALT); under 1.3 low risk of advanced fibrosis (use 2.0 if aged 65 or over), over 3.25 high risk; not validated under 35. Indeterminate results go on to an ELF blood test or FibroScan per NICE NG49 and local pathway. Weight loss of 7 to 10% improved inflammation, and 10% or more improved fibrosis in many patients (Vilar-Gomez 2015). Heart disease, not liver disease, is the commonest cause of death in people with fatty liver. Simplified for explaining results.
