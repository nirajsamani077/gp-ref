Weight Loss Medicines

Weight loss medicines

How semaglutide (Wegovy), tirzepatide (Mounjaro) and the newer tablets work, what to expect, and what to watch for.

Key point

Average weight loss at around 68 to 72 weeks in the main trials alongside lifestyle support: semaglutide 2.4 mg about 15% (STEP 1), tirzepatide 15 mg about 21% (SURMOUNT-1), oral options about 12 to 14% (orforglipron ATTAIN-1, oral semaglutide 25 mg OASIS 4), placebo with lifestyle about 2 to 3%. After stopping semaglutide, people regained about two thirds of the weight lost within a year (STEP 1 extension). Semaglutide cut heart attack, stroke and cardiovascular death by 20% in people with heart disease (SELECT). MHRA: strengthened pancreatitis warning (Jan 2026); effective contraception needed, add a non-oral method for 4 weeks after starting or increasing tirzepatide, avoid in pregnancy and breastfeeding (Jun 2025). NICE lowers BMI thresholds by 2.5 for people of South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean background. NHS England tirzepatide primary care cohorts as published by ICBs; check local criteria. Wegovy tablet authorised in the UK June 2026 and orforglipron (Foundayo) August 2026, neither currently on the NHS.
