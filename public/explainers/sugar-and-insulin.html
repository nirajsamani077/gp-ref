Sugar and Insulin

Sugar, HbA1c and insulin

What your HbA1c number shows, and how insulin resistance builds up over years before diabetes.

Measured by

HbA1c in mmol/mol: under 42 normal, 42 to 47 prediabetes (high risk), 48 or more diabetes (repeat to confirm if no symptoms), per NICE and WHO. Red cells live about 120 days, so HbA1c reflects roughly the last 2 to 3 months, weighted to recent weeks. Less reliable with anaemia, haemoglobin variants, recent blood loss or transfusion, pregnancy and advanced kidney disease. Lifestyle change cut progression to diabetes by 58% in the Diabetes Prevention Program (Knowler 2002). The year-by-year chart is illustrative.
